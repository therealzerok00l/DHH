About The Band

Formed in the heat of the underground music scene, The Dancehall Hooligans merge heavy basslines, infectious rhythms, and raw energy into an unforgettable live experience. Known for turning every venue into a massive dance floor, they are redefining modern dancehall music with a rebellious edge.
